⚠️ Important Notice Regarding Calendar Sync:

This tool produces an independent, static calendar snapshot tailored to your specific teaching schedule. This personal calendar will not auto-update if the school publishes changes to the Bell Schedule (such as an unexpected schedule change or special assembly day).

  • Recommendation: Keep the school’s live Bell Schedule subscribed in your calendar app to receive authoritative schedule updates. Use this customized calendar for setting reminders, custom period titles, personal notes, and room numbers.
  • Handling Schedule Changes: If you spot a discrepancy between the live feed and your personal copy, simply delete your old custom calendar and return here to generate a fresh replacement.

5. Where Your Events Go

Choose which of your Google Calendars gets each group. Most people can leave these as they are.



About your main calendar: these events will show as busy to anyone who can see your calendar, and they'll be mixed in with your own events, so there's no single calendar to delete if you want them gone. Use Remove everything this app added instead. Your own events are never touched.

How this works: unless you change these, the app makes two new calendars, My Classes and School Events, the first time you add events. In Google Calendar you can show, hide, or delete each one on its own.

You can also put both groups on one calendar, or on your main calendar. Either way, the app only ever changes events it added itself. Anything you put in your calendar is left alone.

Changed your mind? Pick a different calendar here and add your events again. They move to the new calendar. The old calendar is left empty, and you can delete it in Google Calendar's settings.
