# Faculty Calendar Builder: beta tester note

A draft message for the first testers. Copy it into an email or chat and fill in the link.
The link is not kept here because this repo is public.

---

Hi! Thanks for trying out the Faculty Calendar Builder. It puts your class periods and the
school events you choose into Google Calendar (or into an .ics file for Apple Calendar or
Outlook), so you don't have to type them in.

**Link:** [paste the /exec link here]

**The first time you open it**, Google asks you to allow access to your calendar. That's
expected: the app needs it to make two new calendars for you, "My Classes" and "School
Events", and put events in them. It runs as you, and only ever changes events it added itself.
Anything you put in your calendar is left alone.

**What to do**

1. Wait a moment for the bell schedule to load (card 1).
2. Pick your dates (card 2). **Tip:** for a first try, pick just two weeks. It's much faster,
   and you can check the result by eye. Change it to the whole year once you're happy.
3. Check the periods you teach and give them new names if you like (card 3). Typing a name
   checks the box for you.
4. Check the kinds of school events you want (card 4). If this card says it can't open the
   faculty calendar, add the faculty calendar to your Google Calendar and reload the page.
   Please tell me if that happens!
5. Card 5: click **Add to my Google Calendar**. You'll see exactly what will change first.
   Nothing happens until you click **Confirm**. Keep the page open until it says it's done.
   Or, if you'd rather not touch your Google Calendar, click **Download .ics file** instead.

Your choices are saved, so next time you open the page they're already filled in. Run it
again whenever you change your picks, and the app updates what it added.

**If you want it gone:** hide or delete the "My Classes" and "School Events" calendars in
Google Calendar, or open the app and use **Remove everything this app added** (at the bottom
of card 5).

**What I'd love to hear**

- Anything confusing, or any wording you had to read twice.
- Whether your periods and school events came out on the right days and times.
- Any error message, word for word (a screenshot is great).
- About how long it took.

Just reply to this message. Thank you!
