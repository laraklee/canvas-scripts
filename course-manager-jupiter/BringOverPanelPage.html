4. What happened

Redo these by hand (copying can't bring them):

    How to redo them
    • Button or picture you drew: in your old copy, click it with the right mouse button (that selects it without running it), press Ctrl+C (⌘C on a Mac), then click a cell on the same tab here and press Ctrl+V. Click the button's ⋮ › Assign script and check the name matches the old one. Or skip the button: every tool is also in the Course Manager Tools menu.
    • Chart: in your old copy, click the chart, press Ctrl+C (⌘C on a Mac), then click a cell on the same tab here and press Ctrl+V. Then click the chart › ⋮ › Edit chart and check its data range points at this sheet's tab.
    • Pivot table: in your old copy, click inside it and note its rows, columns, values and filters in the editor on the right. Here, select the same data and choose Insert › Pivot table, then add the same fields.
    • Protected range: Data › Protect sheets and ranges, then add the same range and who can edit it.
    • Filter view: Data › Filter views › Create new filter view, and set the same filters.
    • Alternating colors: select the range, Format › Alternating colors.
    • Unusual dropdown or color rule: compare Data › Data validation, or Format › Conditional formatting, in both copies.

    Next:

    1. Setup…: save your Canvas token (part 2; a new copy needs it again) and check the course (part 3).
    2. Refresh tables…: tick All and Jupiter categories, then Update. Your Jupiter ticks are kept.
    3. Look over each tab next to your old copy, including its buttons.
    4. Check preview mode in Setup…, part 4 (a new copy starts with it ON).
    5. Rename your old workbook (for example "OLD Course Manager"). Keep it until you're sure everything came over.
