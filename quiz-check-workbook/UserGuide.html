Quiz Check — User Guide

Version 2026-11-24. See 6. What's new and getting updates.

This guide is always at Quiz Check › ❓ User guide. It opens in a panel on the right, so you can follow the steps while you work in the sheet. Close it with the ✕ at its top.

What it does

Checks every quiz in a Canvas course before you give it, looking for the problems that make a test go wrong in class:

  • Pictures students can't open (from another course, someone's personal files, an outside site, an old question-bank file, unpublished, or missing) and pictures big enough to load slowly.
  • Questions with no question text, no correct answer marked, or a blank answer choice.
  • Question groups that pick 0 questions, pick more than they have, or pull from a question bank that isn't there.
  • Points that don't add up to the quiz's total.

It checks Classic Quizzes and New Quizzes, including the questions inside question banks and item banks. It works from a copy of your course that you download from Canvas, so it needs no Canvas password, token, or setup, and it never changes anything in Canvas.

1. Your own copy

Make your own copy of this workbook with File › Make a copy. One copy works for all your courses. The Quiz Check menu appears at the top a few seconds after the workbook opens.

The first time you use the menu, Google asks you to allow the script. It asks for two things only: to view and change this spreadsheet (to write the results) and to show its side panel. It can't see your other files, your email, or Canvas.

2. Download your course from Canvas

  1. In your Canvas course, open Settings and click Export Course Content (on the right).
  2. Choose Course (not Quiz), then Create Export.
  3. Wait for it to finish (a big course can take several minutes; you can leave the page and come back), then click the new export's link to download it. The file ends in .imscc.
Why the whole course, not just the quiz? A single quiz's export is made with your teacher access, so it can pack in pictures that students can't open (for example pictures that still point to last year's course). The course export shows those. A single-quiz export (.zip) still works, but the check will remind you that the course export is stricter.

3. Check the quizzes

  1. Quiz Check › Check quizzes… opens a panel on the right.
  2. Choose file, pick the .imscc you downloaded, then click Check.
  3. After a few seconds to a minute, the Quiz Check tab lists every quiz in the course.

The file is read on your computer. Only the quiz text and the size of each picture go to the workbook; the pictures themselves and the rest of the course never leave your computer. Use a current Chrome, Edge, Safari (16.4 or later), or Firefox. On an iPad, ask for the desktop site.

4. Read the results

The Quiz Check tab

One row per quiz, published or not. Red rows need fixing, orange rows have only suggestions (pictures worth shrinking), and plain rows look fine. Red rows come first. What to look at says what was found and in how many questions, for example picture from another course (3). The tab is replaced each time you check.

Detailed report for one quiz

After the check, the panel lists the quizzes in the same order and colors. Click one and its report goes on the Quiz Check detail tab: one row per question group (gray) and per question, with its choices, correct answer, pictures, and a Check column saying what to fix. Rows to fix are red, rows with only suggestions are orange. The panel stays open, so you can click the next quiz; each click replaces the detail tab (duplicate the tab first if you want to keep one).

5. Good to know

  • The check shows the course as it was when you exported it. After fixing things, make a new export and check again.
  • Nothing in Canvas changes. The workbook never contacts Canvas; you fix things in Canvas yourself.
  • No student information. A course export holds course content only (quizzes, pages, files), never students' names, answers, or grades.
  • Tab names: if you already have a tab called Quiz Check or Quiz Check detail that you use for something else, the check asks you to rename it and changes nothing.
  • If something goes wrong, the panel says what happened. Try once more; for a very large course, try a computer instead of a tablet.

6. What's new and getting updates

Getting updates: your copy doesn't update by itself. When there's a new version, make a fresh copy of the original workbook (File › Make a copy). Your old copy's results aren't needed: each check starts fresh from a new export.
